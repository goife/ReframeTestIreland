What is EMDR Therapy and How Does It Heal?

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is a scientifically validated, world-recognized therapy. Unlike traditional talk therapy, EMDR focuses directly on how your brain stores traumatic or stressful memories.

When overwhelmed by trauma, panic, or chronic stress, the brain’s natural information processing system can get blocked. EMDR uses bilateral stimulation (such as eye movements or rhythmic tones) to help your brain process those memories, neutralizing emotional distress so you feel safe again in the present.

No need to describe events in detail
Faster results than traditional talk therapy
Recognized by WHO & NHS
Highly effective in online formats

Every individual's path to healing is unique. At Reframe (reframe.ie), Adrienne provides tailored EMDR programs for a wide range of emotional challenges.

Process single-incident trauma (accidents, medical events) or complex relational trauma safely, reducing flashbacks, nightmares, and hypervigilance.
